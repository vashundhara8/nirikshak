# Official Government Policy Sources

Purpose:

This directory stores official government documents that
provide the authoritative source material for the MoTA
Scholarship Intelligence & Lifecycle Platform.

The documents are used for:

- policy research
- policy versioning
- rule extraction
- source traceability
- evidence mapping
- policy-engine development
- evaluation

## Source Authority Principle

Only official government documents should be treated as
authoritative policy sources.

Priority:

1. Ministry of Tribal Affairs
2. Government of India
3. Official government departments/agencies
4. Official DBT/NSP sources where applicable

Unofficial websites, blogs, coaching websites, social media,
news articles and third-party scholarship websites must NOT
be treated as authoritative policy sources.
